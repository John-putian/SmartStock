Read this with `REVIEW-AND-TESTS-2026-10-08.md`, which covers the work up to
8 October. This file covers one day: closing the gaps that were still open.

## 1. The photo AI is live

Google Gemini is configured and making real calls. Two drafts produced during
testing:

- `cloisonne-tiger-vase.jpg` → "Dark Blue Ceramic Vase with Tiger and Bamboo
  Illustration", Home Decor, 3.5 s. Price: **₱500, source `demo`, 0 matching
  sales** — explicitly a placeholder.
- `wooden-sideboard-6drawer.jpg` → "Wooden Chest of Drawers with Metal
  Accents", 2.9 s, through the real HTTP endpoint with the page's CSRF token.
  Price: **₱5,510, source `sales_history`, 15 matching sale lines** — the real
  history path.

Those two prices demonstrate the distinction the brief asked for: a demo
placeholder is labelled as one, and a figure from the shop's own sales says so
and says how many lines it rests on.

**The key never travels with the code.** It lives in
`config/photo-ai.local.php`, which is excluded from the zip and denied to the
web by `config/.htaccess`. `config/photo-ai.php` reads it as an override. No
Apache restart is needed when it changes.

## 2. Phone camera photos now work instead of being refused

Before today, an iPhone HEIC file and any camera photo over 6 MB were rejected
outright — by the browser, by PHP, and by the AI service. The picture is now
decoded on the phone and re-encoded as a JPG of at most 1600 px before anything
is uploaded, and that smaller copy is what is saved on the item.

| Given | Result |
|---|---|
| 4000×3000 JPG, 11.6 MB | accepted → 665 KB, 1600×1200 |
| File labelled `image/heic` | converted → `.jpg`, `image/jpeg` |
| 3600×3600, labelled HEIC, through the real endpoint | uploaded as 180 KB JPG; AI returned "Acoustic Guitar with Red Sides", ₱2,200 from 15 sales |
| Small 600×450 JPG, 272 KB | left exactly as it was |
| Unreadable file | "This picture could not be read…", in red, suggest button stays off |

The staff member sees a line such as *"Photo prepared: 11.6 MB → 665 KB
(1600×1200)"*, so nothing happens silently.

**Not proven:** the conversion was tested with JPEG bytes *labelled* HEIC, not
with a real iPhone HEIC file. Safari decodes HEIC natively so it should work;
Chrome on Android cannot, but Android cameras produce JPEG anyway. This needs
one photo from a real iPhone to be certain.

## 3. A page that tells you the phone address

**Help → Open Smart Stock on a phone** (`index.php?page=help.connect`).

The laptop's address on the shop Wi-Fi is handed out by the router and changes.
A written-down address therefore stops working. This page asks the machine for
its address every time it is opened, and shows a QR code drawn by the QR
library already bundled with the project — so it works with no internet.

It also ranks the addresses. A development laptop has several, and most are
useless to a phone: this one found `192.168.56.1` (VirtualBox) *before* the real
Wi-Fi address `192.168.31.15`. Virtual adapters are now pushed to the bottom and
labelled "Probably not this one".

With no network at all it says so rather than showing an empty page.

## 4. The firewall question, answered

Checked on the laptop itself:

- Wi-Fi "ME HOME NEO" is in the **Public** profile.
- An enabled inbound rule **"Apache HTTP Server" covers the Public profile**.
- Apache is listening on `0.0.0.0:80` and `[::]:80`.

So **Windows is already allowing it** — no rule needs adding. The earlier worry
was unfounded.

Two security notes while looking: inbound rules on the **Public** profile also
apply in a café or an airport, and both **Apache and mysqld** have one. The
tidier arrangement is to mark the shop Wi-Fi as Private in Windows and allow
only the Private profile. Changing that is a security setting, so it is left for
you to do.

## 5. What staff see when the AI fails

19 checks, run against the real `PhotoAssistant`. Where a failure needed a real
service on the other end, one was used: the live Google endpoint with a rejected
key, and a local HTTPS service answering with real status codes.

| Situation | What the staff member is told |
|---|---|
| No key configured | "Photo suggestions are not enabled. Ask your administrator… or enter the details manually." |
| Key rejected (real 403 from Google) | "…Ask your administrator to check the AI key and model, or enter the details manually." |
| Service unreachable | "The AI service could not be reached. Try again later or enter the details manually." |
| Real 429 (busy / out of allowance) | "The AI service is busy or its allowance has been reached." |
| Real 500 / 404 | "Photo suggestions are unavailable… or enter the details manually." |
| Two requests inside 15 s | "Please wait 15 seconds before requesting another draft." (in red) |
| Reply the wrong shape — 9 variants | "The photo did not produce usable details…" |
| Unknown category suggested | becomes 0, so a person must choose |
| HTML or injected instructions in the reply | stripped; a draft can never carry a price field |

No message leaks the key or the service's raw text. After a refusal the form
stays fully usable: every field was filled by hand and the form validated with
the AI refusing.

## 7. The reviewed system is now the installed system

The work since 2 October lived in `SmartStock-reviewed` against a copy of the
database. Your real system was still the older code. That is now resolved.

**No database migration was needed, and none was made.** The schema of the live
database was compared against the reviewed one, column by column and index by
index. The result was the opposite of the usual worry: the live database is a
**superset**. It carries `customers`, the `vwproductstock` view,
`sales.status`, `sales.customer_id`, `sales.remarks`, `categories.status`,
`audit_logs.record_id` and `audit_logs.machine_name` — none of which are
referenced by a single line of either codebase. They are leftovers from an
earlier design. Nothing the reviewed code needs was missing.

What was done:

- `C:\xampp\htdocs\SmartStock` copied to
  `Documents\SmartStock-code-backup-2026-10-09` first — 159 files.
- The reviewed code copied over it, keeping the existing
  `config/database.php` so it still points at `aa_inventory`, and bringing the
  AI key file across.
- The duplicated `assets/vendor/vendor` folder and the raw `*-TEST-RESULTS.txt`
  files left out.
- Verified: nothing existed only in the installed copy, so nothing was lost.
  All 64 product photos identical in both.
- 13 pages loaded against the real database with no PHP error or warning:
  dashboard, inventory, alerts, reports, analytics, approvals, audit,
  categories, users, notifications, help, help.connect, pos.

**To undo it:** delete `C:\xampp\htdocs\SmartStock` and rename
`Documents\SmartStock-code-backup-2026-10-09` back to it. The database was never
touched, so there is nothing to undo there.

## 9. Still not proven — read this before saying the system is finished

1. **No phone has ever loaded this system.** Everything points to it working:
   Apache is listening on all interfaces, Windows already allows it, the
   address and QR are correct. But it has not been done once. Open
   **Help → Open Smart Stock on a phone** and scan the code. This is the one
   remaining unknown that matters.
2. **Phone layouts were tested in an emulated 390×844 viewport**, not on a real
   device. Real-device touch, keyboard and camera behaviour can differ.
3. **Real iPhone HEIC bytes** have not been through the converter (see §2).
4. **No test with several people on several tills at the same time.** The
   concurrency tests simulate it; real staff on real phones have not.
5. **Printing has not been tested on your printer** — receipts or QR labels.
6. **The MariaDB crash fix is still unproven over a long run.** It has held
   since 2 October, which is encouraging, not proof.
7. **The API key pasted into chat should be revoked** and replaced.
8. 252 passing checks means 252 things do not break. It is not proof the system
   is correct. Two real defects were found late in this work — an approval
   re-check that would have silently failed, and a cart-fingerprint guard that
   had never once fired.

What is fair to say: *the inventory, sales, approval, decision-support and
photo-drafting flows are verified against a real database, and phone
deployment is prepared and unblocked but not yet confirmed on a handset.*
